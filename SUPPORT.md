# ScreenTimeNext — Support

ScreenTimeNext helps screen time end peacefully. A countdown, a warning before the end, and a
screen that asks your child what they want to do next.

## Get help

- **Email:** guoxiachen@gmail.com — the fastest way to reach me. Please say which iPhone and which

- **Report a problem or request a feature:** [open an issue](https://github.com/guodominic/ScreenTimeNext/issues)

I usually reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**The apps I picked are not being blocked.**
ScreenTimeNext needs Screen Time permission, granted once by a parent. Open the app; if it asks for
Screen Time access, approve it. Then check that at least one app or category is selected under
"Protected content" on the dashboard — nothing is covered until something is picked.

**My child sees a grey "Restricted" screen instead of yours.**
That is iOS's own Screen Time screen, and it means nothing of ours was selected when the block was
raised. Open ScreenTimeNext, tap "Pick apps and categories", and choose at least one.

**Time ran out but nothing happened.**
Restrictions apply only to the apps and categories you selected. If the "App restriction removed"
slider on the dashboard is green, everything is unblocked for the rest of today; it comes back on
its own at midnight.

**Can my child give themselves more time?**
No. Time is only ever added from the parent dashboard inside the app. The screen your child sees
has no way to extend a session.

**Does ScreenTimeNext see which apps my child uses?**
No. iOS hands the app opaque tokens for the apps and categories you picked — the app cannot read
what they are, and never stores, logs or transmits them. There is no account, no server and no
analytics. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
